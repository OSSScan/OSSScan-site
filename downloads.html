After downloading

  1. Verify the SHA-256 checksum matches before unzipping (see below).
  2. Unzip the archive.
  3. Launch OSSScan and run Compatibility Check from the startup dialog to confirm OSSScan’s required tools are installed on your machine and to generate your machine fingerprint.
  4. Click Check Project Coverage… in the Compatibility Check dialog to test a project folder, an existing SBOM, or both — no license required — and see whether OSSScan will produce Strong, Partial, or Limited coverage for it before procuring a license.
